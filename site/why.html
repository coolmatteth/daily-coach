Why I built this

Written by a human.

Every fitness app assumes the problem is discipline. On a GLP-1 the problem is the opposite: appetite is the constraint.

The questions nobody answers in one place are simple. Am I eating enough to keep my muscle? Am I actually keeping it? What should I bring to my prescriber? And is what I am feeling this week normal?

I built a coach for myself first. It sends me one message a day, I reply with what I ate and what I lifted, and on Sunday it tells me how the week went. The part that made it trustworthy was not the model. It was the rules in plain code: a weight steps up only after I have owned it twice, a guess is asked about and never logged, and nothing about my dose comes from anything but my own numbers.

This is the public version of that. It is free, it is early, and it is narrow on purpose: for people on a GLP-1 who lift or want to start. If you want cardio programming or a meal plan, there are better places. If you joined a gym because you read about muscle loss and do not know what to do in it, this is for you.

Protein and lifting are the two things you control. The coach's job is to make both easy to do and honest to see.
